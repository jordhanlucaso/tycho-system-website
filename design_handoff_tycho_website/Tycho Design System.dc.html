The Web Observatory system.

A precision-instrument identity for Tycho Systems — named for the astronomer who mapped the sky by hand. Deep navy skies, technical mono labels, and orbital geometry. Calm, exact, and built to earn trust.

01 / Brand mark

The mark is an orbit and a body — a planet observed. The gold point is the fixed star you navigate by.

05 / Motifs & voice
Orbital geometry

Concentric rings, crosshair ticks, a body on each orbit. Used for hero and decoration — never busy.

Starfield

Faint twinkling points behind dark sections. Mostly azure, ~10% gold. Subtle, never a backdrop spectacle.

Voice

Plain and precise, with light navigation metaphors — chart, map, orbit, course. Confident, never cosmic-cliché.
