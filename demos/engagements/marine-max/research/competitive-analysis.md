### 1.1 The finding that reframes the whole pitch

**Marine Max is older than both of its most visible competitors combined in market
tenure — and invisible to both.**

- Tønsberg & Færder Marineservice: registered **2022**. Website, Facebook, 5 employees.
- Nøtterøy Båtsenter: registered **2023**. Website, web shop, service booking form.
- Marine Max: registered **2005**. No website.

A boat owner searching today concludes that the 2022 firm is the established one and the
2005 firm does not exist. **Search visibility has completely inverted actual market
tenure.** That sentence is the opening line of the sales conversation, and it is verifiable
by anyone in ninety seconds at brreg.no.

### 1.2 A note on MOBIL MARITIME SERVICE (registered 04-04-2025)

A brand-new ENK whose *name* is its positioning: mobile service. If Marine Max does offer
mobile service (`[TO_CONFIRM]`), this is the competitor to watch, because a name that
contains the search term is a durable advantage in local results. If Trond confirms mobile
capability, claiming that ground quickly matters.

## 2. Jarlsø Marina — the one not to fight

**jarlsomarina.no** · Jarlsøveien 50, 3124 Tønsberg · 33 32 88 80 `[V]`

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Positioning** | Full-facility marina and authorised workshop. Infrastructure-led |
| **Primary services** | Authorised Volvo Penta workshop; Marine Travelift to 80 tonnes; heated indoor winter storage; 24/7 fuel (98 + diesel); shop `[V]` |
| **Target customer** | Owners of larger boats needing lifting, berthing and indoor storage |
| **Search visibility** | Strong. Dedicated domain, clear service pages, well-established `[V]` |
| **Local SEO** | Good — real address, hours (workshop man–fre 08–16), phone, service pages `[V]` |
| **Trust signals** | Volvo Penta authorisation, certified mechanics, Travelift capacity, testimonials `[V]` |
| **Conversion mechanism** | **Phone and email only. No booking form.** `[V]` |
| **Enquiry workflow** | Unstructured — customer calls, workshop takes details verbally |
| **Strengths** | Capital assets nobody can replicate quickly. Volvo Penta authorisation. Fuel brings recurring physical footfall |
| **Weaknesses** | No structured enquiry capture. Nothing happens outside 08–16. High-overhead model |
| **Content gaps** | No symptom-led or problem-led content. Everything is facility-led |

**Strategic read: do not compete here.** Travelift, heated halls, a fuel licence and factory
authorisation are capital and contractual assets. A sole-operator ENK cannot and should not
try to match them, and any page implying otherwise would be a lie that collapses on first
contact.

**Where they are beatable:** they are an *institution* — you call during office hours and
take your place in the queue. There is no way to submit a job at 21:00 from the dock. A
customer with a misfiring outboard does not need an 80-tonne Travelift; they need someone to
look at the engine. Jarlsø is built for the big-boat, big-job customer, and everything
smaller is a poor fit for their cost base.

## 3. Nøtterøy Båtsenter AS / Outboardspesialisten — the direct competitor

Registered 10-08-2023 `[V]`. Merged with Outboardspesialisten Marthin AS `[W]`.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Positioning** | Outboard specialist + boat centre. Brand-led ("proud supplier of Mercury/Mercruiser") |
| **Primary services** | Motor and drive service, sales, teak treatment, insurance-damage handling, parts `[W]` |
| **Brands** | Supplier for Mercury and Mercruiser; parts and service for Volvo Penta, Yanmar, Yamaha, Suzuki, Evinrude `[W]` |
| **Target customer** | Outboard owners; buyers of new motors; insurance work |
| **Search visibility** | Good, and rising. Owns the literal term *outboardspesialisten* |
| **Trust signals** | Brand supplier status, web shop, merger heritage, terms and conditions published |
| **Conversion mechanism** | Dedicated **"Bestille service"** page with a form `[V]` |
| **Registered activity** | Note: registry `aktivitet` says *repair of agricultural/forestry, construction and industrial machinery* — not boats. The registry text and the website disagree `[V]` |

### 3.1 Their service form — the exact bar to beat

Fields, in order, all required `[V]`:

1. Båtmerke
2. Type motor (merke og modell)
3. Navn
4. Telefonnummer
5. E-post
6. Melding
7. ☑ Jeg har lest og godtatt standardvilkår

Then a long terms-and-conditions block. **No photo upload. Requires JavaScript** — the page
renders "Please enable JavaScript in your browser to complete this form" without it.

**Critique — where this form loses money:**

- **"Melding" is a free-text void.** The one field that carries the actual problem is
  unstructured. Every enquiry needs a phone call to decode. The form has not saved anyone
  any time; it has just moved the phone call later.
- **No photo.** For a marine problem, one photo of the engine plate answers *merke, modell,
  årsmodell and serial* at once. Asking the customer to type what a camera could capture is
  the single biggest miss.
- **No boat location.** In water, on land, or on a trailer completely determines whether the
  job is even possible and what it costs. Not asked.
- **No urgency signal.** "Engine died today, boat is blocking the ramp" and "book my spring
  service sometime" arrive identically. Nothing can be triaged.
- **All fields required, terms gate up front.** Maximum friction at the exact moment of
  highest intent.
- **JavaScript-dependent.** Fails on a flaky mobile connection at the marina — precisely
  where the acute customer is.

**Opportunity for Marine Max:** this is a solved problem, and it is solved by asking better
questions rather than by better technology. Structured, enumerated fields plus a photo
turn a lead into a job that can be quoted without a phone call.

## 5. Ulviken Motorsenter AS — the brief's premise needed correcting

**ulvikenmotor.no** · Ulvikveien 29, 3150 Tolvsrød · 33 32 53 54 `[V]`

The brief described Ulviken as a marine competitor with "boat/motor sales, spare parts,
e-commerce, service across multiple categories". **That is only partly right, and the
distinction changes the recommendation.**

What they actually are `[V]`: a **motorcycle, garden and power-equipment dealer** — Honda,
Husqvarna, Micore, Tohatsu, Terhi, Vespa, Aprilia. Categories span park and garden
machinery, snow blowers, mopeds, motorcycles, ATVs, and a substantial web shop. Opening
hours man–fre 08–16, tors to 17, lør 10–14 — retail hours, not workshop hours.

Marine relevance is real but **secondary**: they carry Tohatsu (outboards) and Terhi
(boats), and they offer winter storage for boats. `[V]`

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Positioning** | Multi-category motor retailer. Volume and range, not marine specialism |
| **Conversion mechanism** | Web shop + contact form + phone + customer accounts `[V]` |
| **Strengths** | Large review footprint; e-commerce revenue; Saturday opening; multi-season demand (snow blowers in winter, mowers in summer) smooths the year |
| **Weaknesses (as a marine competitor)** | Boats are one line among many. Not a marine specialist and does not read as one |
| **Opportunity for Marine Max** | Specialisation. A customer with a marine-specific problem is not looking for the shop that also sells lawnmowers |

**What is worth stealing — the structure, not the design.** Their multi-season demand
smoothing is the interesting lesson. Note that Marine Max's registered activity also spans
*anleggsmaskiner og landbruksmaskiner* — the same countercyclical idea, if it is still true.
That is `[TO_CONFIRM]`, and it is a strategic question, not a trivia question: it determines
whether the site should be purely marine or marine-led with a machinery line.

### 6.1 Four market-wide gaps

Read the matrix vertically. Four rows are empty or near-empty **across every competitor**:

1. **Nobody captures a structured problem.** Every enquiry route in this market ends in a
   free-text box or a phone call. The workshop learns what the job is only by talking.
2. **Nobody accepts a photo.** In a trade where one picture of an engine plate replaces four
   questions, not one competitor asks for one.
3. **Nobody has a face.** Not one of these sites puts a named human forward. For a
   sole-operator business this is the cheapest available differentiator — and the one a
   larger competitor structurally cannot copy.
4. **Nobody serves the acute customer.** Every site is organised around the *company's*
   service list, not around the *customer's* symptom. Nobody has a page that answers
   "motoren starter ikke".

These four gaps define the entire product design. See `design/marine-max-design-direction.md`.

## 7. Why should a customer choose Marine Max instead?

The brief instructs: if there is insufficient evidence to answer this factually, do not
invent the answer. **We can answer it partially, and we mark the boundary precisely.**

### What we can say today `[V]`

1. **Continuity.** Registered and VAT-active on Nøtterøy since 2005 — longer than the two
   most visible competitors have existed. Verifiable at brreg.no.
2. **You deal with the person doing the work.** An ENK with no registered employees means
   the person who answers is the person who fixes the engine. Nothing is relayed through a
   service desk. This is a structural fact of the company form, not a claim about character.
3. **Local in the strict sense.** Bryggeveien 3B is on Nøtterøy — inside the area served,
   not in a neighbouring municipality.

### What would make the answer decisive — but must come from Trond `[TO_CONFIRM]`

- Which engine brands he actually knows deeply
- Whether he comes to the boat
- Whether he handles the jobs the bigger workshops decline as too small
- Turnaround time versus a five-employee shop's queue
- Whether the machinery capability is live

### What we deliberately do NOT claim

Not "best", not "cheapest", not "most experienced", not "authorised", not "certified", not
"fastest". None is evidenced, and in a small community where customers talk to each other,
an unearned superlative is a liability rather than an asset.

**The honest positioning, and the one the site is built on:**

> Same person on the phone and on the engine. On Nøtterøy since 2005.

Both halves are verifiable. Neither requires Trond to confirm anything. Everything stronger
waits for `questions-for-trond.md`.

## 8. What Marine Max should NOT attempt

| Do not compete on | Because |
| --- | --- |
| Lifting / Travelift capacity | Jarlsø has 80 tonnes and a crane pad. Capital asset |
| Heated indoor winter storage | Requires a building. Capital asset |
| Fuel | Requires a licence and installation |
| Web shop / parts range | Nøtterøy Båtsenter and Ulviken have supplier agreements and stock |
| Staff count / queue capacity | T&F has five people. Marine Max has one |
| Breadth of brands | Do not out-list a dealer. Specialise instead |
| The bare term "marine max" | See `marine-max-research.md` §3 |

**Compete instead on:** findability for symptom-and-place searches, quality of the first
contact, being a named person, and being the workshop that is genuinely on Nøtterøy.
