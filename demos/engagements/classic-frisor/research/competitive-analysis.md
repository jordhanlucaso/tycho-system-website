# Competitive analysis — hairdressing on Nøtterøy

Retrieved 24-08-2026. Registry facts `[V]` are from Enhetsregisteret; web facts `[V]` are
from the sites themselves. Nothing here is copied from a competitor's site into the concept.

## 0. The shape of the market

`[V]` 35 hairdressing units (NACE 96.210) are registered in Færder kommune. Six of them sit
on **Smidsrødveien**, the Teie high street, within roughly a hundred metres of each other.
Classic is at number 15, between Trix at 7 and Teie Barber Shop at 9.

This is not a market short of hairdressers. It is a market short of **findable** ones.

## 1. Bibbis Frisør — the one that does this properly

**Target customer.** Women and families across Vestfold wanting a full treatment
catalogue — colour, extensions, bridal — from an established chain.

**Positioning** `[V]` self-described: *"en gründerbedrift med 4 frisørsalonger i Vestfold"*.
Professional, broad, quality-and-environment focused.

**Locations** `[V]` four: Tønsberg (Torvet), Tolvsrød (Olsrød Park), Horten (Linden Park),
and **Nøtterøy (Kirkeveien)**. `[V]` The Tønsberg flagship registers **9 employees**.

> Correction to the brief: Bibbis is not an off-island competitor reaching for Nøtterøy
> search. It **has a Nøtterøy salon**. But it is at Kirkeveien in the Borgheim area, roughly
> four kilometres from Teie — a different catchment, a car journey, not a high street a
> customer walks past.

**Website** `[V]` `bibbisfrisor.no`, WordPress, Yoast SEO, Google Analytics, Typekit,
Facebook + Instagram. A real, maintained marketing site.

**Booking** `[V]` `/bestill-frisortime/` is a routing page — *"Velg frisørsalong – og bestill
time via vår online booking"* — with a link per salon. Online booking exists and is the
primary CTA.

**Services** `[V]` listed verbatim on their booking page: dameklipp, herreklipp, barneklipp,
skjeggklipp, hårfarge, striper, foliestriper, balayage, aktuelle fargeteknikker,
brudefrisyrer, hair extensions, parykk, sminke, kur, farge vipper, farge bryn, voksing,
keratinbehandling, permanent.

**Prices** `[TO_CONFIRM]` — not found on the pages examined.

**Strengths.** The only competitor with a complete digital funnel: site → salon choice →
online booking. Named service pages (bryllup, hair extensions). Multi-location authority.

**Weaknesses.** The Nøtterøy salon is one entry in a chain page rather than a local
business with its own identity — a customer searching "frisør Teie" is offered a salon four
kilometres away, in a chain's voice. Nothing on the pages examined presents an individual
stylist as a person.

**Opportunity for Classic.** Bibbis owns "the professional chain in Vestfold". It cannot own
"the salon on your street in Teie", because it is not on that street.

## 2. Teie Barber Shop — the nearest and strongest neighbour

**Target customer** `[V]` men. Meta description: *"Hårklipp, skjegg trim, hot towel shave"*.

**Registry** `[V]` TEIE BARBER SHOP AS, founded 2018, **7 employees** — by employee count
the largest hairdressing business on the street.

**Website** `[V]` `teiebarbershop.no`. Genuinely good metadata: Norwegian `lang`,
`og:locale=nb_NO`, canonical, a specific description, keyword targeting on "Teie, Nøtterøy,
Tønsberg".

**But** `[V]` it is a client-rendered SPA. The served HTML is a 1.5 KB shell — `<div
id="root"></div>` and a module script. **Every word of content, every service, every price
and every booking control requires JavaScript to exist at all.** Google can render JS, but
it is slower, less reliable, and it means the page has nothing for any other crawler,
preview, or assistive path that does not run scripts.

**Strengths.** Two doors from Classic. Seven staff. Strong review footprint (per the brief).
Clear single-segment positioning.

**Weaknesses.** Its entire content is behind a JS render. Being a barber shop is also a
boundary: it is *not* competing for colour, balayage, or women's cutting.

**Opportunity for Classic.** Do not fight it for men's cutting on its own street. **The
served-HTML gap is the technical opening** — a static, instantly-readable page for the same
searches is a straightforward advantage.

## 3. Trix Frisør — the incumbent, invisible online

`[V]` TRIX FRISØR AS, Smidsrødveien 7 — **three doors from Classic**. Founded 1998,
registered 1999, MVA-registered, still filing accounts (2025). Purpose: *"Frisørtjenester,
salg av produkter…"*.

`[V]` No website found at the obvious domain. `[W]` The brief notes directory data placing
Trix "around the same Smidsrødveien location" — the registry shows why: **it is on the same
short street, at a different number.** There is no evidence of any relationship between
Trix and Classic, and none is claimed.

**Strengths.** Twenty-eight years of local trading and the word-of-mouth that comes with it.
Sells products.

**Weaknesses.** No discoverable owned web presence. A customer who searches rather than
asks a neighbour may never encounter it.

**Opportunity for Classic.** This is the clearest illustration of the market gap: the
longest-established salon on the street is the hardest one to find online. Classic cannot
buy twenty-eight years. It can be the one that turns up when someone searches.

### Four gaps nobody in this market is filling

1. **A local salon page that is actually readable.** One competitor has a real site; the
   nearest one hides its content behind a render.
2. **Prices before you call.** Not found published anywhere in this set. It is the question
   customers most want answered and least often get.
3. **A named person.** Every one of these is a small business built on individuals, and none
   of the examined pages introduces one.
4. **Booking that starts on the page you landed on.** Bibbis routes you through a chain
   selector first. Everyone else, as far as public evidence shows, expects a phone call.

## 7. What can Classic do better without becoming a chain?

Honestly, and only from evidence:

**It can be the easiest salon on Smidsrødveien to find, understand and book.** That is
available today and no competitor on that street currently holds it.

**It can answer the price question in public.** Requires the owner's prices — but it is a
decision, not an investment.

**It can be a person rather than a brand.** A one-chair business is a disadvantage on
capacity and an advantage on trust — *the same person cuts your hair every time* is
something Bibbis structurally cannot say.

**It can be the fastest page in the market.** Static HTML against a competitor SPA is a
measurable, defensible technical edge.

### What Classic must NOT try to compete on

- **Heritage.** Trix has twenty-eight years, three doors away.
- **Capacity or breadth.** Teie Barber Shop has seven staff; Bibbis has four salons and a
  twenty-item treatment list.
- **Bridal, extensions, wigs, lashes, waxing.** Bibbis owns this catalogue. Claiming any of
  it unverified would be both a lie and a losing fight.
- **The bare term "classic frisør".** Three businesses share the name nationally and the
  Askøy one owns the matching domain. Lead with place.
