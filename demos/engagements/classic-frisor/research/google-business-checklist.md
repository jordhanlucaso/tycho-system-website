# Google Business Profile — checklist

**No external account is touched by this project.** This is the plan to hand over, not a
set of changes made.

## Order of operations — this matters

The profile cannot be corrected until the identity question is answered. Editing the name
on a profile that may belong to a different legal entity makes the ambiguity worse.

```
1. Owner confirms legal entity, name, address, phone, hours   ← questions-for-owner.md §1–2
2. Claim / verify ownership of the listing
3. Correct NAP so it is byte-identical with the website
4. Add website URL and booking link
5. Photos
6. Services and prices
7. Review process
8. Only then: publish HairSalon structured data on the site
```

Step 8 is last on purpose. Structured data that contradicts the profile is worse than no
structured data.

## Photos

Google weights recency. A profile with photos from this month outranks a dormant one with
better photos from three years ago.

- Cover: exterior with signage, so a customer recognises the door from the street
- Interior: the chair and the light
- At work: hands and hair, no faces needed
- Team: one portrait, if the owner agrees
- **Cadence: two or three photos a month.** Not a launch dump.

---

## Reviews

`[W]` One review today. Neighbouring competitors have more.

The process, kept simple deliberately:

1. Appointment finishes
2. A short delay — same evening or next morning, not while they are still paying
3. One message with a direct review link
4. Owner replies to every review, good or bad, within a few days

**Never** offer a discount for a review — against Google's policy and grounds for removal.
**Never** write review text for a customer. **Never** publish a testimonial that was not
given.

A realistic target is five to ten genuine reviews over a season. That is enough to change
how the listing reads, and it is achievable by asking.

## Ongoing

- One Google Post a month is plenty — a treatment, a seasonal note, a holiday closure
- Answer questions in the Q&A section rather than leaving them to other users
- Update hours **before** holidays, not after
- Keep NAP identical everywhere. One changed abbreviation is a different address to a machine
